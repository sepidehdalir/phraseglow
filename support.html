Phraseglow support

Type a message, tap Show big text, and use Turn to rotate the message toward someone opposite. Tap Edit to return. Long messages can be scrolled; nothing is removed from your draft.

Speech

Choose an installed voice in Speech & settings. This does not translate. Check your volume and current audio output. Speech can play in Silent mode. Voice availability and offline playback depend on installed system resources. Speech stops when the app backgrounds or audio is interrupted; tap Speak to restart.

Lifetime Pro

Display, speech, all palettes and three saved phrases are free. Pro adds more saved phrases, named collections and named saved styles with a one-time purchase. Use Restore purchases with the Apple Account that made the purchase. Existing saved content stays accessible if your entitlement changes.

Saved data

Keep the app open if a save error appears, free storage and tap Retry saving. If a library cannot be read safely, its original file is preserved. Contact support before reinstalling: uninstalling deletes local data. There is no cloud account or automatic cross-device library sync.

Contact

Email hello@arvinify.com with your device, OS version and what happened. Please omit sensitive message text.
