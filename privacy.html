Privacy policy

Effective October18,2026. Phraseglow is made by Sepideh Javanbakht Dalir / Arvinify.

Your messages stay on your device

The developer does not collect your typed messages, saved phrases, collections, styles, voice selections, identifiers, location or usage analytics. There are no accounts, ads, tracking SDKs or product servers.

Local storage and Apple services

Phraseglow stores your draft and phrase library locally. Device backups depend on your Apple settings. Deleting the app deletes its local library. System speech uses Apple’s installed voices; availability and offline playback depend on the resources installed on your device. Phraseglow does not record audio or request microphone access. Apple processes App Store purchases and any diagnostics you choose to share under Apple’s own policies.

Support

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send. Please avoid sending private or sensitive phrases. We use correspondence to answer your request and do not sell it.
